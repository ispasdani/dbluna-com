# Diligence and fundraising checklist

This is a collection and review checklist. Store evidence in a private, access-controlled data room with a verified owner and update date per folder. The repo holds planning drafts.

| Folder | Files to collect | Status or owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 Overview | Investor one-pager, pitch deck, company overview, contact details, synthetic demo link | One-pager and slide deck drafted. Founder to verify claims, add contact details and approve a demo link. |
| 02 Corporate | Formation documents, register, material resolutions, subsidiaries, shareholder agreements and ownership list | Founder and counsel. Entity and jurisdiction to confirm. |
| 03 Capitalization | Fully diluted cap table, option and warrant ledger, existing financing instruments, investor and key approval rights | Founder and counsel. Reconcile to signed records. |
| 04 Financials | Monthly actuals, bank and billing reconciliation, cash balance, liabilities, budget, forecasts and assumptions | Finance owner. Model is illustrative until actuals are loaded. |
| 05 Traction and sales | Anonymized cohort reports, pipeline, invoices, signed customer contracts and approved references | Founder or sales lead. Verify paid assertions against billing. |
| 06 Product and IP | Demo, architecture, roadmap, IP assignments, third-party license inventory, domains, trademarks and vendor contracts | Engineering and counsel. Validate ownership and availability before diligence. |
| 07 Security and privacy | Data flow, subprocessors, retention/deletion behavior, incident response, backup and restore tests, customer terms and approved agreements | Engineering and counsel. Collect evidence for any commercial promise. |
| 08 Team | Founder bios, organization chart, contractor agreements, IP and confidentiality coverage, milestone hiring plan | Founder. Share personally identifiable details only with appropriate access. |
| 09 Financing | Proposed ask and instrument, use of funds by milestone, downside runway, anticipated dilution and decision approvals | Founder and advisers. Pending strategy and model calibration. |

## Evidence checklist

- Reconcile subscriptions to billing and cash collection. Separate active paid, trial, waived, cancelled and one-time revenue.
- For each traction claim, record date, denominator, cohort, source and approval to publish.
- Trace production import data flow from browser extraction to API processing and any storage or logs. Confirm schema metadata, credentials and customer business notes are handled as described.
- Run authenticated import, handoff export, cloud reload, version restore and access tests on a disposable environment. Save dated reproductions and outcomes.
- Review export disclosure and contract language with the customer. A downloaded HTML file can be forwarded, so sharing depends on the approved recipient and channel.
- Verify licensing obligations and founder or contractor assignment coverage with the appropriate owner. Obtain local specialist review for corporate, privacy and financing documents once jurisdiction is known.

## Fundraising gates

1. **Commercial evidence.** Find repeated paid use or a documented reason that internal adoption follows a different path.
2. **Cash decision.** Update opening cash, monthly costs and customer contribution. Tie the ask to the downside cash gap, contingency and the milestones that funding enables.
3. **Claims review.** Founder confirms pricing, team, product availability, market calculations, KPI provenance and permissions.
4. **Distribution.** Share the brief and synthetic demo first. Grant restricted access to detailed evidence by request and keep a record of recipients and versions.

Use the next monthly review to decide whether to continue bootstrapping, assemble a targeted investor list, or defer fundraising pending evidence.
