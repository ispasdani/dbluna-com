# DBLuna investor brief

Working draft · 15 November 2026

**DBLuna helps teams turn an unfamiliar database into a usable reporting handoff.**

## One-pager

**Problem.** Companies outsource Power BI work while database knowledge remains with internal developers. The consultant has to piece together which tables to use, how to join them and how the business defines its metrics. Errors often surface only when someone checks the totals.

**Product.** DBLuna imports database schema metadata and generates searchable documentation. The report owner adds the business question, grain and rules, reviews the selected sources, and exports a linked HTML handoff. The recipient can open it offline. The source team controls the separate arrangement for data access.

**Initial customer.** Small Power BI consultancies that run multiple SQL Server engagements and can standardize project intake. Initial distribution is founder-led through direct outreach, references and a reusable technical demo. Expansion into internal teams depends on evidence of repeated use.

**Revenue model.** Existing Pro subscription with proposed paid pilots and agency workspace tests. Pro pricing code defines 10 monthly or 100 annually. Live checkout economics and willingness to pay remain to be confirmed. The team measures retained subscriptions separately from paid pilot services.

**Evidence.** The repository contains the core reporting workflow, and a Microsoft sample BACPAC was parsed into 48 tables and 98 resolved foreign keys. This is technical evidence. Verified usage, customer outcomes, revenue, founder bios, legal entity, cash and funding details are pending confirmation.

**Near-term goals.** Measure time to first reconciled metric in three completed pilots, secure two paid pilots, and find two repeat paid engagements before investing in a wider sales motion. These are goals.

**Capital.** No ask has been set. Decide whether to raise after confirming the cost of reaching repeat paid use and the downside cash need. Add the amount, instrument, use of funds and milestone horizon once approved.
